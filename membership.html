Know what you’re getting.

How does checkout work?

Sign up, choose a plan, then pay through Stripe. Your membership activates only after Stripe confirms payment. The merchant account is Only One Worker.

Can I cancel?

Use Membership to stop the next software renewal. Access lasts through the period you paid for. Cancellation does not automatically issue a refund. Training only expires 12 months after payment and does not renew.

What is available today?

The software currently supports a hosted rehearsal of the rental process. Renter charges, coverage checks, and demo signatures are not live payment, insurance, identity verification, or legal approval. The training includes 33 written lessons and worksheets; lessons 1.1 and 1.2 have sample videos. Additional videos and state-specific rules/checklists are still in development.

Will my existing workspace change?

Existing workspaces keep their current access and saved records. New paid workspaces use the plan limits shown above. Worksheet answers remain saved in the browser where you enter them; use the worksheet download/print controls to keep a copy.
