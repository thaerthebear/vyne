Plan Your Rental Business · Lesson 1.4

Understand Your Costs

Your output: Cost and break-even worksheet.

Read lessonDo worksheet
Reading lesson · 197 words

A rental rate is not your profit. First separate the costs that continue each month from costs that change with rentals.

Build three lists: startup spending, recurring fixed costs, and variable costs. Record whether each number comes from a quote, a record, or an estimate. For a simplified operating break-even exercise, subtract variable cost per paid rental day from the daily price. Then divide monthly fixed costs by that contribution. Use the same time period and cost basis throughout. This worksheet is a planning model, not a complete tax or accounting statement.

Use fictional numbers: six hundred dollars in monthly fixed costs, fifty dollars per paid day, and twenty dollars in variable cost per paid day. Each paid day contributes thirty dollars, so the simplified break-even point is twenty paid days. At only fifteen paid days, contribution is four hundred fifty dollars: one hundred fifty short of those fixed costs. Startup recovery, taxes, financing treatment and unexpected costs need separate consideration.
